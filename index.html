Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions before starting? Here are answers to what clients ask most often.

How long does a typical project take?

Turnaround depends on the service. Logo and poster designs usually take 2 to 4 business days. Tech setup sessions are often completed within 24 hours. Full web design projects typically take 1 to 3 weeks based on project size.

What if I need changes or revisions?

Every design and web project includes revision rounds. We review drafts together and adjust colors, fonts, or layouts until you are completely confident with the final result.

How do we get started working together?

It's easy! Simply click "Start Your Project", fill out the short contact form with your ideas, and I will reply within 24 hours with clear next steps and a free estimate.

What files will I receive upon project completion?

For design work, you will receive full high-resolution print files (PDF, EPS) and digital assets (PNG, SVG). For web projects, you get full ownership of your clean code and live website files.
